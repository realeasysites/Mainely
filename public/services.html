Insulation and weatherization services

Spray foam

Spray foam expands as it's applied, filling every gap and crack it touches. It air-seals and insulates in one step, which makes it the go-to for awkward spaces where drafts hide.

We're spray foam certified, and we'll match the foam to the space so you get the most out of every inch.

Good for

  • Rim joists and sill plates
  • Basement and crawl space walls
  • Cathedral ceilings and roof lines
  • New construction and additions
Request a spray foam estimate

Dense-pack cellulose

An eco-friendly insulation blown tightly into closed wall cavities. Packed dense, it slows heat loss, cuts down on drafts and quiets outside noise.

It's installed through small holes, so older homes with empty or settled walls get insulated without tearing out plaster or drywall.

Good for

  • Older homes with uninsulated walls
  • Walls where old insulation has settled
  • Attic floors
Request a cellulose estimate

Fiberglass insulation

Made from glass fibers, fiberglass is a cost-effective way to add solid thermal resistance and soundproofing.

It's a reliable fit for open framing, where it can be installed cleanly and completely before walls close up.

Good for

  • Open walls during renovations
  • Attics
  • Additions and new builds
  • Sound control between rooms
Request a fiberglass estimate

Air sealing

Air sealing blocks the drafts and gaps that let warm air escape and cold air in. You feel it right away: fewer cold spots and less outside noise.

It's usually the first step before adding insulation, because insulation can't stop air that's moving straight past it.

Good for

  • Attic floors and top plates
  • Wiring, plumbing and chimney gaps
  • Attic hatches and pull-down stairs
  • Rim joists
Request an air sealing estimate

Ventilation

Good ventilation circulates fresh air and carries moisture and pollutants out of the house, for better indoor air and more comfort.

Insulation without ventilation leads to condensation. When we tighten up a house, we make sure moisture still has a way out, so attics stay dry and insulation keeps working.

Good for

  • Attics and roof lines
  • Homes after air sealing
  • Houses with damp or stuffy rooms
Request a ventilation estimate

Vapor barrier

A vapor barrier stops moisture from working its way into walls and floors. That protects the house from mold and water damage and helps insulation perform the way it should.

Good for

  • Crawl spaces
  • Basements
  • Walls and floors over damp ground
Request a vapor barrier estimate
