Four quick answers and Scott will call you back.

Insulation without ventilation leads to condensation.

Warm indoor air carries moisture. Seal a house up tight without giving that moisture somewhere to go, and it condenses inside walls and attics. That's how mold, rot and ice dams start. So we plan every job as a system, not just a product.

Air seal

Close the gaps at top plates, wiring and plumbing holes, attic hatches and rim joists so warm air stays inside.

Insulate

Use the right material for the space: spray foam, dense-pack cellulose or fiberglass.

Ventilate

Give moisture a way out with proper attic and home ventilation, so the insulation stays dry and keeps working.

What we install

Most jobs combine two or three of these. Not sure what your house needs? Ask, and we'll tell you what will make the biggest difference.

  • Spray foam

    Expands as it's sprayed, filling gaps and cracks so it air-seals and insulates in one pass.

    Good forRim joists, basements, cathedral ceilings, new construction

    See spray foam jobs

  • Dense-pack cellulose

    An eco-friendly fiber blown tightly into closed walls through small holes, so walls don't have to come down. Cuts drafts and outside noise.

    Good forOlder homes with empty walls, attic floors

    See cellulose jobs

  • Fiberglass

    A cost-effective choice with solid thermal and sound performance.

    Good forOpen walls, attics, additions, new builds

    See fiberglass jobs

  • Air sealing

    Blocks the drafts and leaks that let heat out and cold in. Often the most noticeable change per dollar.

    Good forAttics, top plates, hatches, wiring and plumbing holes

    See air sealing jobs

  • Ventilation

    Moves fresh air through the house and carries moisture and pollutants out, so a tighter house stays healthy.

    Good forAttics and roof lines, homes after air sealing

    See ventilation jobs

  • Vapor barrier

    Keeps moisture out of walls and floors, protecting against mold and water damage and helping insulation do its job.

    Good forCrawl spaces, basements, walls and floors

    See vapor barrier jobs
